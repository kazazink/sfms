SFMS

SFMS is the school finance app of Ranao Child Development Center.

Privacy

SFMS uses Google sign-in only to keep the school's own files in the school's own Google Drive. It can open only the files it created there, and nothing else in the account.

SFMS does not sell or share any data and does not use it for advertising. The school can remove SFMS's access at any time in its Google account settings.
