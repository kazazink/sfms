SFMS

SFMS is the school finance app of Ranao Child Development Center, Marawi City. It runs on the school's own computer and keeps the school's records there.

Privacy

SFMS asks Google only for permission to create and read its own files in the school's Google Drive (the folder "SFMS sync"). It cannot see any other file in that Drive, the school's email, or any other Google data.

Every copy is encrypted on the school's computer before it is sent. The key is the school's printed recovery key, which never leaves the school. Google stores the copies but cannot read them.

SFMS sends nothing to anyone else. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. The school can remove SFMS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
